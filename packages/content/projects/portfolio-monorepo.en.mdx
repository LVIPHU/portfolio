## Problem

A portfolio that should last years: redesigning must not mean re-entering bio, posts, or photos. Two (then more) versions need to ship in parallel from one source of truth.

## Approach

A pnpm + Turborepo monorepo. Each year is an app under `apps/`; data, MDX, UI, i18n, and API live in `packages/`. The 2026 and 2025 apps share the Next 16 + React 19 lock.

## Outcome

One change in `packages/content` lands on both sites. Blog MDX falls back across locales. View stats share `@portfolio/service`.
